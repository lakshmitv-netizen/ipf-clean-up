How a cell decides what to render

The value-cell render path is renderCellValue(monthKey) in GridRow.tsx:2552. States are evaluated in strict precedence — the first match wins:

Risk→ Locked→ Read-only→ Editing (input)→ Directly edited→ Impacted→ Saved-edited→ Normal
Conditional formatting, selection, focus and hover are overlays — they layer on top of whichever base state above is active, rather than replacing it.
