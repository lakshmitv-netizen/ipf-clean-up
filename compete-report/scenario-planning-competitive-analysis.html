01 Four classes, four definitions of “scenario”

The word “scenario” means genuinely different things across these products, and conflating them produces nonsense comparisons. In a planning platform a scenario is a modelled version of the future with its own driver assumptions. In revenue intelligence it is usually a forecast category — commit versus best case — not a modelled alternative at all.
