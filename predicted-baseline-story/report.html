The takeaway

From "where am I" to "where is this heading — and what's at risk"

What the Predicted Baseline adds

A committed sum is a snapshot of today. The Predicted Baseline projects each node forward — exposing which parts of the book are accelerating (Midwest EV) and which are steady (Southwest legacy) — so the plan reflects momentum, not just commitments.

How the numbers are built

The baseline is derived off the committed forecast for every SKU, lifted by a plant-level ramp learned from history, then rolled up bottom-up. It never drops below the committed floor, and rolls to 14.73M for FY26 vs the committed 13.84M.
